Why Panosphere

Made for the part of college nobody organizes for you.

College life doesn’t arrive in one neat format. It shows up in inboxes, course portals, group chats, paper flyers, and last-minute announcements. Panosphere is an idea for a calmer layer across all of it: a space that feels closer to a well-loved journal than a corporate calendar.

Less catching up. More being there.
